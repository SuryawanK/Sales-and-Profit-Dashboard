# Business Problem

  1. Which Produk category contributes the most to sales and profit?
  2. Why the furniture category needs to be evaluated?
  3. What products cause business losses?
  4. When did the highest and lowest sales occur?
  5. How can a company  increase its profit?

# Insights

  1. The technology category has the highest sales at 704.45 thousand dollar, with a profit of 121.86 thousand dollar. Meanwhile. Furniture generated 622.34 thousand dollar in sales, but its profit was only 17.89 thousand dollar.
  2. Furniture generates substansial sales, yet its profit margin is only around 2.87%. This indicates that high sales volume does not necessarily translate into optimal profit.
  3. The "Tables" subcategory incurred the largest loss at 12.33 thousand, followed by "Bookcases" at 2.43 thousand and "Supplies" at 198.51. This means that these three subcategories generated negative profit, despite producing sales.
  4. Based on monthly data aggreagtion, December recorded the highest sales at 241.46 thousand dollar, while February saw the lowest at 59.64 thousand dollar.
  5. Technology and office supplies generated profits of 121.86 thousand dollar and 108.21 thousand dollar, respectively. In contrast, furniture yielded a profit of only 17.89 thousand dollar from 622.34 thousand dollar in sales.


# Recommendation

  1. Maintain and increase sales in the technology category.
  2. Evaluate discount strategis and operational costs.
  3. Focus on Furniture products that yield a positive profit.
  4. Audit the selling prices, products costs, and discounts for the tables category.
  5. Prepare stock and promotional strategis well in advance for high-sales periods
  6. Prioritize the growth of categories with strong profitability.
